TeenWork

The teen job app for safe local jobs, ages 13–17

TeenWork is a free teen job finder for lawn care, pet sitting, snow shoveling and other neighborhood jobs. A parent approves every application, and chats with employers stay inside the app.

Parents approve every application · Free · No cut of pay

How it works

A teen can look at jobs right away, but can't apply for any of them until a parent is linked and agrees.

01

The teen signs up

Teens 13–17 make a free account with their age, town and skills, then browse jobs near them.

02

A parent links and agrees

The teen shares a code. A parent makes their own account, links to the teen and agrees to let them apply.

03

The parent approves each application

Every application waits for the parent. The employer only sees it after the parent taps Approve.

04

Chat, work, get paid

Teen and employer plan the job in TeenWork's chat. Pay is between them, and TeenWork doesn't take a cut.

For teens

For employers

Safety, in plain words

What TeenWork checks, and what it doesn't. No app can make a job safe on its own, so parents stay involved at every step.

👪

Parent approval

A linked parent has to agree before a teen can apply, then approve each application before the employer sees it.

💬

Chats checked before sending

Messages between teens and employers go through a word filter and AI checks first. AI can miss things, and if a check is down, messages still send after the word filter.

🚨

“Something feels wrong?” button

In any chat, it tries to notify the teen's linked parent and the TeenWork team. It isn't an emergency line: if someone is in danger, call 911.

⚖️

Work rules by age and state

Applying checks the job's minimum age, jobs teens aren't allowed to do, and whether the teen's state needs a work permit. Employers still have to follow the law.

🔍

Employers aren't background-checked

Employers sign up with an email and password. TeenWork doesn't verify who they are or run criminal background checks yet, so meet the employer first.

🛡️ How safety on TeenWork works

Written in plain words, October 2026. No safeguard is perfect, so please read the limits too.

What TeenWork checks

  • Teens type in their own name and date of birth. TeenWork doesn't check IDs. A teen can't apply for a job with a higher minimum age.
  • Parents make their own, separate account. Before a teen can apply, a parent links to the teen with the teen's code and agrees. TeenWork doesn't check IDs, so it can't prove the person is really the teen's parent.
  • Employers sign up with an email and password and describe their job. TeenWork does not verify who employers are or run criminal background checks right now. Meet the employer before the first job, with a parent if you can.
  • Jobs: when a teen applies, TeenWork checks the job's minimum age, job types teens aren't allowed to do (like construction, or farm machinery under 16), and whether the teen's state needs a work permit. If it does, the teen uploads a photo of the permit, an AI reads it, and unclear ones wait for a person to look. Employers are still responsible for following child labor law, including hour limits.

Chats

  • Teens and employers message inside TeenWork, so there's a record. Before a message is sent it goes through a word filter, a check for moving the conversation off TeenWork (phone numbers, other apps, paying cash) and for harmful content (OpenAI's moderation check), and an AI review of the last few messages for an adult trying to get a teen alone or around their parents (Google Gemini, with Groq as a backup).
  • Blocked messages aren't delivered. Chats the AI flags are saved for the TeenWork team to look at.
  • What it can miss: filters and AI make mistakes both ways. They can't see anything said outside TeenWork, and if a check can't be reached, the message still goes through after the word filter.
  • Parents don't see their teen's chats automatically. Talk with your teen about who they're messaging.

Alerts

  • In any chat, a teen can tap the red “Something feels wrong?” button. It emails their linked parent (if one is linked) and sends the note to the TeenWork team's inbox.
  • The TeenWork team is small and doesn't watch that inbox around the clock, so it is not for emergencies.

In an emergency

  • If anyone is in danger, call 911.
  • If an adult asks you to keep a secret, meet alone, get in their car or send photos: stop replying, tap “Something feels wrong?”, and tell a parent or another adult you trust.
  • You can also call the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline, 24/7: 1-800-422-4453.

Your data and privacy

  • Accounts, job posts, applications and messages are stored in TeenWork's database (Supabase) so they work on any device.
  • To check chats, the text of recent messages (without names or emails) is sent to AI services: OpenAI's moderation check, Google Gemini and Groq. Work permit photos are sent to Google Gemini to be read and aren't kept. These services have their own privacy terms.
  • TeenWork doesn't sell personal data. You can delete your account from the Danger zone in your profile or settings, and download your records first.

📄 Terms of Use & Privacy

Last updated: April 2025 · Version 2.0

1. Who Can Use TeenWork

TeenWork is a supervised teen employment platform for teens ages 13–17, their parents/guardians (18+), and employers posting legal age-appropriate jobs. By creating an account you confirm you meet the eligibility requirements for your account type and that all information you provide is accurate and truthful.

2. Parent & Guardian Accounts

Parent accounts must be created by an adult (18+). Creating a parent account as a minor to bypass oversight violates these terms and may result in permanent suspension. We use date-of-birth verification and knowledge questions to enforce this. Parents bear ultimate responsibility for their teen's job activity on this platform.

3. Teen Worker Accounts

Teen accounts require a linked parent/guardian. Parents retain full oversight and approval authority over all job applications, messaging, and profile activity. Teens may not disable or circumvent parental controls. TeenWork may share relevant activity summaries with linked parent accounts at any time.

4. Employer Responsibilities

Employers must comply with all applicable federal and state child labor laws. Specifically:

  • All job postings must accurately describe duties, pay, and time requirements.
  • Employers may not require teens under 16 to operate heavy machinery, work past 10pm on school nights, or exceed legal hour limits.
  • Employers must obtain and retain any required work permits or employment certificates.
  • Misrepresentation of job duties, pay, or working conditions is grounds for immediate account termination.
  • Employers may not request any information from teens that is not strictly related to job performance.

5. Safety & Child Protection

TeenWork automatically scans all messages for inappropriate, harmful, or predatory content. Blocked messages are logged and may be reviewed by our safety team. Patterns of concerning behavior are reported to the appropriate authorities. We maintain zero tolerance for grooming, harassment, or exploitation of minors.

6. Data Collection & Privacy

We collect name, email, age, location (city/state), skills, and job activity data necessary to operate the platform. We do not sell personal data. We share only what is required for job matching: for teens, employers see first name, age, city, and skills only — never contact info, photos, or full legal name until a job is parent-approved. Parent contact info is never shared with employers.

7. Push & Email Notifications

By creating an account you agree to receive transactional emails (account verification, job updates, safety alerts). You may optionally enable push notifications for new job matches, application status updates, and platform activity. You can adjust notification preferences at any time in your account settings. If you are inactive for 7+ days, we may send you a re-engagement email to let you know about new jobs or activity in your area. You can opt out of these emails at any time.

8. AI Features

TeenWork uses AI to assist with job matching, message safety screening, and informational responses. AI responses are informational only and do not constitute legal, financial, or professional advice. AI language checks are used to review message content for safety — this is a mandatory feature that cannot be disabled for accounts involving minors.

9. Location Data

We use your city and state to match you with nearby jobs. We do not collect GPS or precise location data. You may update your location at any time in your profile. Location data is used only for job matching and is never shared with third parties for advertising.

10. Account Activity & Re-engagement

If your account has been inactive for 7 or more days, TeenWork may send you an in-app message and/or email to notify you of new opportunities, nearby job activity, or platform updates relevant to your profile. These messages are sent to help you not miss opportunities — not for advertising purposes. You can opt out in settings.

11. Termination
